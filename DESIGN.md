# Design System: Music Studio KH

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Listening Room"**

A calm room built for one activity: listening. Surfaces are quiet and slightly warm,
the mint accent appears only where an action or a selection lives, and nothing on the
screen competes with the music. Density is moderate — a list you can scan, a player
bar that stays put, generous space between groups and tight space inside them.

The room is layered rather than flat: panels sit above the page on soft shadows, the
player floats at the bottom, and the sidebar is its own plane. Depth is a hierarchy
device, never decoration. Nothing moves unless the person moves it.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three theme choices only: System, Light, Dark.
- One accent (harbor mint) for primary action, selection and live state only.
- Layered planes: page, panel, raised control, floating player.
- Inter at a small, tight scale; labels in uppercase micro-caps for grouping.
- Rounded, quiet controls (7–14px) with pill shapes reserved for chips and status.
- Legible in dark and light, following the Mac by default.

## Colors

A two-plane palette: deep sea-ink surfaces in dark mode, warm paper in light mode,
with one mint voice that never covers more than a small part of a screen.

### Primary
- **Harbor Mint** (#386e49 light / #b9dfb9 dark): the single accent. Primary buttons,
  the selected navigation row, active filters, the current track, focus rings.
- **Mint Wash** (#dce9df light / #253d31 dark): the accent's quiet companion for
  selected-row backgrounds, chips and hover fills. Never used as a text colour on
  large surfaces.

### Neutral
- **Sea Ink** (#111b21): dark-mode page background.
- **Deep Panel** (#18252c) and **Raised Panel** (#1e3038): dark-mode cards and controls.
- **Paper** (#f4f5f0): light-mode page background.
- **White Panel** (#ffffff) and **Raised Paper** (#e7ede7): light-mode cards and controls.
- **Ink Text** (#f0f3ed dark / #142b31 light): body copy and headings.
- **Harbor Muted** (#8fa4aa dark / #5d7175 light): secondary copy, metadata, placeholders.
- **Status extras** (used only for state): error ink #8f2b23 (light) / #f3b0a4 (dark);
  amber #dfb87e and blue #a5bdeb for provider/source tags, with #ff9f43 as
  the download accent. Each has a translucent wash variant (#e6b77818, #879fd51b) and
  never substitutes for Harbor Mint on a primary action.
- **Hairline Border** (10–12% white on dark, 9% ink on light): the only divider.

**The One Voice Rule.** Harbor Mint is used for the primary action, the current
selection and live state. Everything else is neutral; if two things on a screen are
mint, one of them is wrong.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system)
**Body Font:** Inter (same stack)
**Label Font:** Inter, uppercase at micro size

**Character:** One family, tightly scaled. It reads as a tool, not a magazine: the
page title carries the only large type, everything else is small and steady.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, 25px, 1.15): the page title on each screen, one per view.
- **Title** (600, 20px): section headings inside a screen and dialog titles.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5): list rows, descriptions, form values.
- **Label** (600, 11px, 0.08em, uppercase): group headers in the sidebar and small
  field labels. Never used above a page title.
- **Micro** (600, 10px): chip text and dense metadata; **Small** (400, 12px): hints
  and footnotes. **Hero** (700, 38px): the sign-in headline only.

**The Quiet Scale Rule.** The working ramp is 9 · 10 · 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 16 · 18 ·
20 · 25px, with display 22–38px for screen titles and 47–80px only for the sign-in
hero. A new size outside that set is a new decision; prefer weight and colour first.
Monospace appears twice (log/technical readouts) and never as general body text.

## Layout

A two-plane shell: a fixed sidebar (about 230px) and a content column that takes the
rest, with the player bar floating across the bottom of the content area. Content is
capped by generous side padding rather than a hard max-width; on narrow windows the
sidebar collapses to icons and the player keeps its controls on one line.

Groups are separated generously (22–32px) while items inside a group sit tight (4–8px),
so the eye can find a block without reading it.

## Elevation & Depth

Layered by design: panels sit above the page on one soft shadow, and the player bar
floats highest because it stays with the person. Shadows are wide and weak, never
crisp, so they read as light rather than outline.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Panel lift** (`0 12px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` light / `#0003` dark): cards, dialogs,
  the floating player.

**The Floating Player Rule.** Only the player and true overlays float. A list row or a
card in the page flow gets a hairline border, never a shadow.

## Shapes

Soft rectangles: 7–8px for inputs and small controls, 9px for buttons and nav rows,
11px for cards, 14–18px for dialogs and sheets, 3–6px for tiny marks. Pills (999px)
are reserved for status chips and the version button. Circles only for icon buttons,
avatars and transport controls. Borders are hairlines at 9–12% ink; no heavy strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 9px radius, 10px 16px padding, 14px label.
- **Primary:** Harbor Mint fill with white text (light) or mint wash with mint text
  (dark). One per view, for the action the screen exists for.
- **Secondary:** raised panel fill with ink text; used beside the primary.
- **Hover / Focus:** background shifts one step (mint wash ↔ mint); focus is a 2px mint
  ring offset from the control, never a colour change alone.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity with the pointer cursor suppressed and no hover shift.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, mint wash fill with mint text when selected; hairline border and
  muted text when not.
- **State:** selection is carried by fill plus `aria-pressed`, not by colour alone.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 11px (14px for dialogs).
- **Background:** panel colour; raised controls inside use the raised tone.
- **Shadow Strategy:** panel lift only for floating layers (see Elevation).
- **Border:** hairline; cards are never nested inside cards.
- **Internal Padding:** 22px, 14px between rows.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** raised panel fill, hairline border, 7px radius, 10px 12px padding.
- **Focus:** mint ring plus a solid border; the caret is mint.
- **Error:** the message names the problem and the fix, in the error colour with an
  icon; the field keeps its border change after the message appears.

### Navigation
- **Style:** sidebar list with a leading icon and a 14px label; group headers in
  uppercase micro-caps. Active row: mint wash fill with mint text and a 9px radius.
  Hover: raised panel fill. The footer holds Support, the version pill and the update
  controls, flush to the bottom edge.

### Player bar
- **Style:** floating panel with panel lift, transport controls on the left, title and
  seek bar in the middle, secondary actions on the right. It never scrolls away and
  never covers the last row of a list.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Harbor Mint to one primary action, the current selection and live state.
- **Do** use the panel/raised pair for hierarchy instead of new colours.
- **Do** give every control default, hover, focus, active, disabled and error states.
- **Do** show a hairline border on in-flow cards and the panel lift on floating ones.
- **Do** write empty states that invite the next action ("Choose music folder").
- **Do** use tabular numerals for durations, counts and byte sizes.

### Don't:
- **Don't** nest a card inside a card, or stack two shadows.
- **Don't** put an uppercase kicker above a heading, or use uppercase for anything but
  micro-labels.
- **Don't** use gradient text, glass, blur, decorative motion or page-load choreography.
- **Don't** use emoji, Unicode glyphs or monospace as icons or "technical" costume.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent colour, a second type family or a new radius step
  without deciding it here first.
